I had often look back on my young self as a bad person.

Though I can never really explain why.

My friend told me that I was too harsh on myself and there are things that I need to cut myself some slack for.

And I think they make perfectly logical sense, and also something that I believe that I believe in.

I want to convince myself that I am logical as a person, but ultimately I do have to accept that I work fundamentally as an emotional being and that does not make it bad, neither does it make me any different from the rest.

Looking back on my past and being aware of the the shortcomings is a blessing. Maybe it means that I have grown and if I dont have an ounce of penance for the past me, then it means that I may have stagnated as a person.

That will not stop me from looking back fondly on the memories of old, I think it did make me the person that I am today, and by my standards, I dont think I am a bad person :]

The past me was a menace, but I hope the future me will look upon him with the eyes of a doting adult.
