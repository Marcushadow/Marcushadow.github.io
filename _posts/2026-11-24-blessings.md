With the hardships of life, I think it is often easy for me to forget how lucky I am to be where I am.

Many challenges in life will stay, but maybe some will not.

I know that as a person I am not considered dumb. 

It does make me feel alone sometimes when people take it for granted that things in my life pan out the way I want it to as a result.

Maybe it does add additional pressure. Maybe it does add additional loneliness. Maybe it does add additional stress.

But maybe I am lucky to be faced with this problem. Maybe some others would love to be where I am today. 

Maybe it makes me unrelatable as a person.

I sure hope not.

The fear of isolation trumps all, and I think no one is exempt from that. Maybe.

Solitude can be a blessing, a retreat into my thoughts.

But stay too long and maybe I never return the same.

But maybe it has helped me better understand myself enough to know how I think. 

And maybe I can be a better person for my friends and family when they need me to be there.

Maybe I should go and study now.

Maybe.
